About Farheen

A Medical Technologist focused on clinical and surgical technology, perioperative support, and patient safety.

Farheen Muhammad Arif is a Medical Technologist currently working at Aga Khan University Hospital (AKUH). She manages complex instrumentation and clinical technology for Neurological Procedures, facilitates seamless technical workflows in General Surgical and Orthopedic Surgeries, and ensures specialized technical preparation for advanced Gynecological Procedures.

She completed her Graduate House Officer training at Dow University O.T. Complex (Jul–Dec 2024), gaining extensive intraoperative competence in Neurological and General Surgical protocols as well as Plastic and high-turnover Gynecological setups. She also served as Graduate House Officer at SMBB Trauma Centre, delivering fast-paced operational assistance during trauma interventions including Craniotomy, Laminectomy, and V.P. Shunt installations.

She holds a BS in Medical Technology from Dow University of Health Sciences (DUHS) with a CGPA of 3.9/4.0 and is currently pursuing an MPhil in Medical Technology.
